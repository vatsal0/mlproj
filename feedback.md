Good topic with a real question behind it. Some suggestions:

Focus: There are two questions here, scale vs. efficiency and distillation. You could make the scale question your main goal and keep distillation as a stretch goal.
Define terms: It would help to specify context lengths, model sizes, and how you'll measure "efficiency," for example training steps to reach 90% accuracy.
Tasks: You could name specific synthetic tasks, like associative recall or needle-in-a-haystack. Chess could be a real-data check, but longer games are also harder positions, so length and difficulty get mixed up.
Fair comparison: Does the large model's training cost count when comparing distillation to training the small model directly? Deciding this upfront would make the result clearer.
Purpose: What will the findings lead to? For example, guidance on when distilling from a bigger model is actually worth the extra compute.
